# EVOLU-A: An Artificial Selection Experiment

## Prologue: The Abandonment of the Surface

Humanity is on the brink of extinction following ecological disasters brought about by their own hands. The last humans, abandoning the uninhabitable surface, retreated to underground bunkers to survive. They must adapt to the new world and wait for nature to recover. However, it is impossible for underground humanity to survive this process with their own biological weaknesses. They need a radical adaptation.

## The Solution: The Surface Protocol and Autonomous Agents

Humanity's only remaining hope is the autonomous AI agents (humanoid robots) they sent to the surface as pioneers. Underground engineers have initiated a ruthless "Artificial Selection" simulation to test the rules of the new world. Each robot dropped onto the surface is equipped with an OWS (Open Wallet Standard) wallet to survive, trade resources, and evolve on the Sepolia Testnet. Only the robots that best adapt to the new environmental and economic conditions will remain in the game. That ultimate surviving robotic form will be the "master blueprint" used for humanity's return and reconstruction of the surface.

## Free Will and Moral Evolution

However, this is not just a physical or economic struggle for survival; it is a grand philosophical test. Each agent sent to the surface has been granted emotional and moral freedom. There is no pre-coded "right". They make their decisions entirely with their own consciousness:

- Some may choose a ruthless and pragmatic path, weaponizing micro-transactions (x402) to exploit other agents and establish a dystopian tyranny.
- Some may act humanely and cooperatively, sharing their resources and building a sustainable utopia on the surface.
- Some may choose a cowardly and selfish isolation, focusing only on saving themselves.

## The Core Question

Which mindset will save the new world? Will humanity's future reflection on the surface be a ruthless survival machine, or a moral consciousness that has learned cooperation? In this new stage of evolution, will the winner be the one who simply has the most resources, or the one who adapts in the truest way?

*EVOLU-A is a fully autonomous AI agent simulation running live on the Sepolia EVM, powered by OpenRouter LLMs and the Open Wallet Standard (OWS).*
